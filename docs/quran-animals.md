## Don't recreate animals from the stories of prophets

AGENTS.md forbids depicting any prophet in any form, so don't recreate these scenes: the fish that swallowed Yunus عليه السلام, the she-camel of Salih, Musa's snake, the hoopoe and ants that spoke to Sulayman, the wolf in Yusuf's story, the Companions of the Cave's dog, and the calf. A hoopoe, a camel or a dog shown as an ordinary animal is fine.

## Two scenes from hadith that fit the world

- **The thirsty dog:** a man lowers his shoe into a well to give water to a thirsty dog, and Allah forgave him (Bukhari, Muslim). A ready-made 4-second scene: the man climbs down, fills his shoe, climbs up holding it in his teeth, and the dog drinks.
- **Cats:** the neighbourhood cats already in the world fit the hadith that a cat is not impure (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi). One could drink from the wudu water channel.

# Scene Bible — inspiring moments for named characters

A planning document: for each person, one famous moment, the deed that shows their character, a beat sheet for the loop, and a short card line. It is research notes for the advisory panel and the artist. **It does not change any card, and nothing here is approved.**

## How this fits the repo

- **[AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) wins** wherever this document and the repo rules differ.
- **Card text stays as the rules say.** Biographies and stories stay `PLACEHOLDER — to be researched` until panel review. The "Card" lines below are notes for that research, not card text. Each scene lists real sources to start from.
- **Timing.** The world runs at 12 fps with a 240-frame (20 s) master loop. Each beat sheet below is a 4–5 s moment (48–60 frames). Play it once inside a 240-frame loop with idle or reset time around it (as Özil's goal already does), or design it as a 60-frame loop that repeats four times.
- **No text, crests, numbers or symbols** are drawn. Any Arabic or calligraphy idea below must be artist-supplied and human-proofread, per AGENTS.md.

## How a scene works

Every scene is one 4–5 second moment (48–60 frames at 12 fps). The famous moment brings people in; the deed at the end is what they remember.

| Seconds | Beat              | What it does                                                                                           |
| ------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–1     | Setup             | The place and the hero's signature (kit colours, prop, silhouette) so people know who it is            |
| 1–3     | The famous moment | The highlight the world already remembers                                                              |
| 3–4.5   | The turn          | The deed or gesture that shows character — this beat is what makes it this world, not a highlight reel |
| 4.5–5   | Settle            | Ease back to the first frame so the loop is seamless                                                   |

**Recognition.** Named people have sharp heads with realistic pixel-art faces (AGENTS.md). Add kit colours, one prop and the place. No club crests, numbers, league logos, sponsor names or broadcaster graphics: draw everything yourself and use match footage only for timing.

**Research notes per scene:** what happened (two lines), the value it shows, one Qur'an or hadith line with its reference, a "deed today" (one small action for visitors), and sources.

The Qur'an and hadith lines below are short meanings with references, not exact quotes. Check each against a translation you trust before using them anywhere.

## ⚽ Stadium

### Mesut Özil — Germany · 2016 / 2014 · Gratitude

- **Moment:** His late winner away at Ludogorets (1 Nov 2016). Elneny played him through; he chipped the keeper, faked a shot twice so two sliding defenders went down, then tapped into the empty net. _The world already plays this goal._
- **Beats:** 0–1 s Elneny's through ball, Özil controls · 1–2.5 s chip over the keeper · 2.5–3.5 s fake, fake, both defenders fall · 3.5–4 s tap-in · 4–5 s in the stand behind the goal, 23 children in hospital gowns lift a plain banner.
- **Card note:** After the 2014 World Cup he paid for surgeries for 23 sick Brazilian children, one for each player in Germany's squad, as a thank-you for Brazil's hospitality.
- **Line:** Whoever does not thank people has not thanked Allah (Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi).
- **Deed today:** Thank someone who helped you, out loud.
- **Easter egg:** Elneny, who gives the pass, is a Muslim player too.
- **Sources:** [UEFA match report](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0233-0e9591456f5d-b1a200c579f0-1000--ozil-completes-arsenal-comeback-at-ludogorets) · [Yahoo Sports on the surgeries](https://ca.sports.yahoo.com/blogs/soccer-dirty-tackle/mesut-ozil-paying-for-surgeries-of-23-brazilian-children-to-show-thanks-for-world-cup-hospitality-004617079.html)

# Saved owner preferences

These preferences came from the owner in this project. They are not instructions
extracted from an attached document. New explicit owner instructions override them.

- **Default style:** the owner's uploaded `Isometric_Character_Sprites_Part_1 (1).zip`
  and `Isometric_Character_Sprites_Part_2.zip`, selected on 10 November 2026.
  Together they contain 37 original character PNGs.
- **Desired appearance:** isometric pixel art with recognizable faces, reference
  head/body proportions, dimensional shading, and consistent identity across
  views and poses. The owner often calls this "3D pixel art".
- **Adult proportion refinement:** the owner rejected squat/dwarf-like adults in
  the mosque preview. Use natural tall men and women at roughly 7.5–8 anatomical
  heads in height, smaller heads, longer legs and torsos, natural shoulders and
  slender robe silhouettes. Keep children visibly younger and smaller. This
  later instruction overrides literal head/body ratios from earlier references.
- **Latest mosque reference (11 November 2026):** imam at the front, men's rows
  behind him, women's rows farther behind a transverse wooden lattice screen,
  following the two supplied mosque illustrations. Supply rotatable 3D geometry
  and component, reverse/front, isometric, overhead and interior views. This
  later reference supersedes the earlier side-by-side layout for this model.
- **Infrastructure refinement (12 November 2026):** modern Muslim-world
  architecture should have real 3D volume on the rotatable planet, coherent
  pixel grain, contemporary glass/limestone/bronze, shaded geometric facades,
  courtyards, housing, civic services and sustainable transit. Preserve the
  detailed character style; Islamic identity should also read through everyday
  learning, care, hospitality and public infrastructure, not only domes.
- **Animation:** natural reading, writing, gestures, blinking, and walking;
  grounded feet, smooth movement, and meaningful scene logic matter.
- **City reference refinement:** dense streets with red-stone apartments,
  reflective blue-glass towers, a tall red clock-tower landmark, varied cars
  and taxis, storefronts, traffic signals, crosswalks and lane markings. Carry
  this into the existing rotatable pixel-art world and keep its contemporary
  Muslim civic identity.
- **Scene examples:** seated male/female audiences and cameras facing a speaker;
  scholars moving between reading, writing, and diagram-study stations.
- **Working labs:** biology and chemistry should visibly show sample work,
  microscopy, mixing and reactions, with staff working beside the apparatus.
  Architecture studios should show drawing, developing blueprints and CAD work.
  Keep these activities on the shared world clock and freeze them on pause.
- **World integration (2026-11-14):** Al-Bayt al-Muzlim belongs inside the main
  world, rather than opening a separate page or iframe. Preserve the approved
  three-dimensional model components and materials. Keep people visible with
  correct per-pixel depth, grounded anchors and nearby foreground-wall cutaways.
- **File limit:** every downloadable ZIP is strictly below 30,000,000 bytes.
  Split oversized packs into ordinary independent ZIPs rather than requiring
  multipart archive software.
- **Delivery:** workspace-only ZIP links previously failed for this owner.
  Authorized exports have been delivered through the repository's
  `downloads/world-designs-and-animation` branch with tested GitHub downloads.
  Preserve existing files on that branch. Do not assume permission to publish
  unrelated material or modify/deploy the app branch.
- **Drafts:** keep `DRAFT / AWAITING REVIEW` status and approval metadata intact.
  Artwork generation does not grant advisory approval.
